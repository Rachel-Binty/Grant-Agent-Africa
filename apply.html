Step 0

Which grant are you applying for?

0 of 6 sections completed

Section 1 of 6

Organisation Overview

Write about your organisation:
• What is the name of your organisation and when was it founded?
• What is your mission or main purpose?
• How many people does your organisation serve or employ?
• What sector do you operate in and in which country?
Section 2 of 6

Problem Statement

Describe the problem you are solving:
• What specific problem or challenge does your community or sector face?
• Who is most affected by this problem?
• What evidence or data shows this problem exists?
• Why has this problem not been solved yet?
Section 3 of 6

Your Solution

Explain your solution:
• What product, service or programme do you offer to solve this problem?
• How does your solution work step by step?
• What makes your approach different from what already exists?
• How long have you been running this solution?
Section 4 of 6

Impact & Results

Share your impact so far:
• How many people have you helped or reached so far?
• What specific results or outcomes have you achieved?
• Do you have any numbers, statistics or testimonials?
• What impact do you expect to achieve with this grant funding?
Section 5 of 6

Budget Plan

Explain how you will use the funds:
• How much funding are you requesting in total?
• List the main budget items (e.g. equipment, salaries, training, marketing).
• What percentage goes to each area?
• Do you have any other funding sources or co-funding?
Section 6 of 6

Team & Capacity

Tell us about your team:
• Who are the key people leading this project?
• What relevant experience or qualifications do they have?
• Do you have any partnerships or support from other organisations?
• Why is your team the right one to execute this project?
